# LLM Zoomcamp

LLM Zoomcamp is a free, hands-on course on building real-world applications with Large Language Models. You use hosted LLMs to build, evaluate, and monitor production-style RAG systems, working through agentic RAG, vector search, orchestration, evaluation, and monitoring before finishing with a capstone project.

LLMs themselves are treated as black boxes: the course is for people who want to build with LLMs rather than train them. Each cohort runs once a year and lasts about ten weeks, and all materials stay available for self-paced study in between.
